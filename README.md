# MGS — Project ATLAS

### Meridian Global Shipping | Data Analytics Project

##  Project Overview

MGS — Project ATLAS is a practical shipping analytics project developed using **Microsoft Excel**.

The project analyzes 1,000 shipment records to understand financial performance, vessel and customer performance, trade-lane costs, delivery delays, fuel costs and CO₂ emissions.

The objective is to transform raw operational data into meaningful business insights and an interactive management dashboard.

---

##  Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering key business questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit is being generated?
- Which vessels generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue and profit?
- Which trade lanes are most profitable?
- Which trade lanes have higher fuel costs?
- What are the most common reasons for shipment delays?
- Which customers experience higher delays?
- What is the company's delivery performance?
- What is the overall fuel and CO₂ impact?
